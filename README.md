# ShareSolar 🌞

## Overview
**ShareSolar** is a community-driven platform that enables households to **share rooftop solar energy**, split savings fairly, and reduce reliance on fossil fuels.  
Built during a hackathon, this project demonstrates how AI + backend services can empower climate resilience.

---

## Problem
- Many households generate solar power but cannot fully utilize it.
- Neighbors without panels miss out on clean energy benefits.
- Fossil fuel dependence continues to harm air quality and climate.

---

## Solution
ShareSolar provides:
- 📊 **Data simulation** of household solar generation and usage.
- ⚡ **Fair sharing logic** to distribute excess energy.
- 🖥️ **FastAPI backend** for processing and APIs.
- 🎙️ **Voice-enabled frontend** (Whisper + gTTS) for accessibility.
- ☁️ **Firebase integration** for storage and collaboration.

---
## Impact
- 🌍 Reduces fossil fuel dependence.  
- 💨 Improves air quality.  
- 🤝 Builds climate resilience through community sharing.
